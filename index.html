Case Study #1 • B2B Workflow SaaS

FlowSync AI Landing Page Optimization

📈 Result: +3.2x Signup Conversion Rate
❌ BEFORE (High Friction)
  • Jargon Headline: "Empowering Enterprise Synergy Through Next-Gen Orchestration."
  • Form Friction: Required 6 mandatory input fields for a simple demo.
  • Low Contrast CTA: Dark gray button hidden in dark background.

  • Outcome Headline: "Automate Cross-App Workflows in 3 Clicks."
